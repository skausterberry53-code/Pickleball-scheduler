Court access and pairings are gender-neutral. The scheduler focuses on fair sit-outs, avoiding consecutive breaks, protecting second-hour arrivals only from an immediate first-round sit-out, and reducing repeated partners and opponents. In the second hour, new arrivals are protected from sitting out in the first round where possible. Everyone still receives a fair first break; if second breaks are needed, they normally fall to two-hour players before one-hour second-session players.
